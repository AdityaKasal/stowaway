Unzip the one for your computer and double-click `stowaway`.

| Download | For |
|---|---|
| `stowaway-windows.zip` | Windows 10/11, x64 |
| `stowaway-mac.zip` | Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer), macOS 13+ |
| `stowaway-mac-intel.zip` | Intel Macs, macOS 12+ |
| `stowaway-linux.zip` | x64 Linux with glibc 2.34+ (Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Fedora 35+) |

What's new:
- **Every speed estimate in the menu is now measured**, not guessed: all four models at 4 GB, ~5 GB free (a typical 8 GB Windows laptop), 8 GB and 16 GB, on NVMe and SATA drives. For example, with 16 GB gpt-oss-120b runs at ~4 words/s and gpt-oss-20b at ~14 (RESULTS.md, section 24).
