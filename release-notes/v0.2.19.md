What's new:
- **New top model: `qwen3.8-next`** (Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, 125B, Unsloth's 4-bit, 94 GB). Its makers' tests put it ahead of models 2-3 times its size. It uses only 6B of its parameters per word, so it runs at ~2.6 words/s with 8 GB of memory and ~4 with 16 GB, about as fast as gpt-oss-120b. It's recommended when you have ~9 GB or more free. Its 29 GB word-pattern table stays on disk and is read a few rows at a time (RESULTS.md, section 29).
- The Qwen3.5-122B entries are no longer offered in the menu (Qwen3.8 is stronger and faster); if you already downloaded one, it still shows and runs.
- `--fast` is ignored for Qwen3.8, as for gpt-oss: swapping its experts changes the answers too much.
- The download speed shown while downloading was wrong (it drifted toward 1 MB/s); fixed.
