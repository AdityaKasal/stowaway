Unzip the one for your computer and double-click `stowaway`.

| Download | For |
|---|---|
| `stowaway-windows.zip` | Windows 10/11, x64 |
| `stowaway-mac.zip` | Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer), macOS 13+ |
| `stowaway-mac-intel.zip` | Intel Macs, macOS 12+ |
| `stowaway-linux.zip` | x64 Linux with glibc 2.34+ (Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Fedora 35+) |
| `stowaway-linux-arm64.zip` | 64-bit ARM Linux: Raspberry Pi 5 and 4, ARM Chromebooks, ARM laptops |

What's new:
- **No more "one-time setup" after downloading.** Models from the menu now download straight into the layout stowaway reads fast. Every byte is written to your drive once instead of twice (for gpt-oss-120b that's ~60 GB less writing), and the minutes-long setup step is gone. The result is byte-identical to the old route, still checked against Hugging Face's checksum, and an interrupted download picks up where it stopped.
- **Fixed on Windows:** the old setup step was also writing zeros over the whole packed file before the real data (tens of GB of pointless SSD writes for the big models). Models you bring yourself still use that step, now without the extra writes.
