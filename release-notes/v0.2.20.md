Unzip the one for your computer and double-click `stowaway`.

| Download | For |
|---|---|
| `stowaway-windows.zip` | Windows 10/11, x64 |
| `stowaway-mac.zip` | Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer), macOS 13+ |
| `stowaway-mac-intel.zip` | Intel Macs, macOS 12+ |
| `stowaway-linux.zip` | x64 Linux with glibc 2.34+ (Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Fedora 35+) |
| `stowaway-linux-arm64.zip` | 64-bit ARM Linux: Raspberry Pi 5 and 4, ARM Chromebooks, ARM laptops |

What's new:
- **Qwen3.8 is faster with 12 GB of memory or more** (~3.7 words/s with 12 GB, from ~3.3; ~4.1-4.3 with 16 GB, from ~3.9). stowaway loads the experts the next layer will probably need while the current one computes, but that guessing was switched off for Qwen3.8 because its layers are built differently from the other models'. It's on now whenever the memory cache is deep enough for it to pay. With 8 GB the drive is the limit and nothing changes; other models are unchanged (RESULTS.md, section 30).
