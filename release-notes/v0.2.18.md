What's new:
- **A faster Qwen 122B option: `qwen3.5-122b-4bit`** (Unsloth's dynamic 4-bit, 60 GB instead of 92). With 16 GB of memory it runs at ~3.4 words/s, twice the Q5 version, at a measured, small quality cost: the same top word as the near-original Q8 95% of the time, versus 97% for Q5 (RESULTS.md, section 28). The Q5 version stays the default for Qwen 122B. On 8 GB machines, gpt-oss-120b is still the better big model (~2.7 words/s at full quality).
