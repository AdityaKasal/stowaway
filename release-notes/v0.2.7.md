Unzip the one for your computer and double-click `stowaway`.

What's new:
- **OpenAI's gpt-oss models.** `stowaway run gpt-oss-120b` gives you a 120-billion-parameter model on an 8 GB, no-GPU laptop at ~2.3 words per second (about 4x the Qwen 122B at Q8 there). `gpt-oss-20b` does ~7-9 words/s on 8 GB and ~2.4 on 4 GB. The output is identical to plain llama.cpp.
- **`--fast` is exact when it doesn't swap.** It now keeps the model's own order of experts, so its output only changes where it actually swaps an expert. `--fast` is not used for gpt-oss: measured, swapping their experts changes the answers too much (details in RESULTS.md, section 22).
- gpt-oss thinks briefly by default (`--think` for more).
