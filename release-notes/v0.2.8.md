Unzip the one for your computer and double-click `stowaway`.

| Download | For |
|---|---|
| `stowaway-windows.zip` | Windows 10/11, x64 |
| `stowaway-mac.zip` | Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer), macOS 13+ |
| `stowaway-mac-intel.zip` | Intel Macs, macOS 12+ |
| `stowaway-linux.zip` | x64 Linux with glibc 2.34+ (Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Fedora 35+) |

What's new:
- **It recommends a model for your computer.** The menu shows how many words per second each model should give on *this* machine, based on its free memory and drive speed and on measurements from 4, 8 and 16 GB test machines with NVMe and SATA drives. It marks the best one that's still comfortable to read (about 3+ words/s) and fits on your disk. Press Enter to take it. Typically: 4 GB -> gpt-oss-20b, 8 GB -> Qwen3.5-35B, 16 GB -> gpt-oss-120b.
- `stowaway list` shows the same per-computer speeds.
- Measured on a SATA SSD (0.55 GB/s) with 8 GB: Qwen3.5-35B 2.5 words/s, gpt-oss-20b 1.9, gpt-oss-120b 0.5 (RESULTS.md, section 23).
